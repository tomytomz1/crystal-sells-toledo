# A2P 10DLC error 30882 remediation — SMS-specific legal pages

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** source remediation prepared; Campaign has **not** been resubmitted yet.

## What happened

Twilio rejected Campaign `CM3425248ff3928f6f9c78894afe908ae6` with error
**30882 — Campaign vetting rejection: Terms & Conditions**. The already-approved
Primary Customer Profile and Sole Proprietor Brand remain approved. The rejected
Campaign remains the only A2P Campaign for this program and is eligible for edit
and resubmission.

Twilio's current 30882 documentation says this rejection can result from a missing
or unsuitable Terms & Conditions URL, prohibited affiliate / third-party lead
marketing, policy language that appears to share or sell consumer or mobile
opt-in data to third parties or affiliates, consent that is not direct and
campaign-specific, or another messaging-policy conflict.

Twilio's current A2P onboarding guide also explicitly recommends considering
**messaging-specific privacy policies and terms** because they are easier to
review and keep current.

## Why the remediation is narrow

The first submission used the site's broad legal pages:

- `/privacy`
- `/communications-terms`

Those pages serve more than the SMS campaign. The Privacy Policy legitimately
discusses non-SMS website processors and transaction-related sharing, and the
Communications Terms also cover the separate automated / AI voice permission.
Those statements are not affiliate marketing, but they make the A2P reviewer
parse unrelated policy language to determine what applies to SMS.

The remediation does **not** weaken or delete the broad legal pages. Instead it
adds a dedicated SMS-only pair whose scope is exactly the registered campaign.

## New review surfaces

### `/sms-privacy`

Title / H1: **Privacy Policy**.

It is limited to the Crystal Sells Toledo SMS program and states:

- what SMS consent and messaging information is collected;
- how the information is used for inquiry-related customer care and compliance;
- which operational processors act on Crystal's behalf;
- the exact sentence: **"We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal
  information with third parties for marketing purposes."**;
- mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or
  affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes;
- STOP / HELP, message frequency and message/data-rate disclosures;
- no title-company, lender, inspector, AI-voice, affiliate-marketing or lead-sale
  ambiguity.

### `/sms-terms`

Title / H1: **Terms & Conditions** with a visible **SMS Terms** section.

It is limited to the Crystal Sells Toledo SMS program and contains:

- program / brand name and first-party customer-care purpose;
- message frequency varies;
- message and data rates may apply;
- **Reply STOP to opt out** and **Reply HELP for help**;
- Crystal's customer-care phone and email;
- **"Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages."**;
- SMS consent is optional and not a condition of service;
- no third-party lead generation, affiliate marketing, list rental or unrelated
  promotional blast use;
- a direct link to `/sms-privacy`.

## Consent surface

The canonical SMS disclosure text and version are **unchanged**. No historical
consent record is rewritten.

The shared consent block now adds explicit adjacent links labelled **SMS program**
to `/sms-privacy` and `/sms-terms`. The existing canonical disclosure remains
server-owned and byte-stable, and the separate AI-voice checkbox continues to use
its existing broader legal disclosure.

## Build / test contract

Both new pages carry the existing `communications_consent` feature gate:

- consent OFF: neither page is built or added to the sitemap;
- consent ON: both pages are built, indexed, cross-linked, and linked from every
  opt-in form surface.

`tests/a2p-sms-legal.test.mjs` exercises the real static build in both states and
asserts the A2P-required content from rendered output rather than source-only
presence.

## What this does not prove

- It does not prove that Twilio will approve a resubmission.
- It does not change or resubmit the Twilio Campaign.
- It does not activate outbound SMS.
- It does not merge or activate Gate 8.
- It does not change the separate AI-voice consent or terms.

## Operator sequence after merge

1. Let Vercel Production deploy the merged source.
2. Verify live, rendered `/sms-privacy` and `/sms-terms` against the requirements
   above.
3. Open the rejected Campaign's **Edit & resubmit** flow.
4. Keep the existing Sole Proprietor use case, first-party campaign description,
   web-form-only consent method, sample messages and message-content declarations
   unless Twilio presents a new field-level issue.
5. Change the Campaign URLs to:
   - Privacy Policy: `https://crystalsellstoledo.com/sms-privacy`
   - Terms & Conditions: `https://crystalsellstoledo.com/sms-terms`
6. Re-run Twilio's Campaign pre-check.
7. Resubmit only after the review screen shows those exact URLs.

A resubmission is still a review request, not approval. Automated outbound SMS
must remain disabled until Twilio marks the Campaign approved and Gate 8 is live.
