Newer construction, open plans and modern finishes. Ask how a home here compares with nearby resales and any new construction available.

Mature trees, generous lots and established streets. Compare the lot, layout, condition and updates of each home.

Homes near Fort Meigs and the Maumee River. Check access and views for each individual property.
