Why bother asking a person

Your home may have details an online estimate misses.

An online estimate depends on the data available to it, and may not reflect recent work or the full condition of your home. Tell Crystal about updates, repairs and anything the available records may miss — a new roof, a finished basement, or what the lot backs up to.

In a market as varied as this one — where a century home in the Old West End, a new build in Perrysburg and a ranch on an acre in Sylvania all sit within a short drive of each other — an estimate can change when the available information does not reflect the home’s condition, updates or relevant recent sales. A personal review helps identify those gaps.
