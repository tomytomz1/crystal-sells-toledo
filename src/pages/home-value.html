Why bother asking a person

Online estimates miss the things that decide your price.

Automated valuations are a starting point and nothing more. They cannot see the new roof, the finished basement, the fact that your lot backs onto trees instead of the collector road, or the kitchen you redid in 2023.

In a market as varied as this one — where a century home in the Old West End, a new build in Perrysburg and a ranch on an acre in Sylvania all sit within a short drive of each other — that gap is routinely tens of thousands of dollars in either direction.
