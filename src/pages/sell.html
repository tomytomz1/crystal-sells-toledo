The honest version

Three things to work through before you list

Preparation and pricing choices deserve attention before your home goes on the market.

Listing before it is ready

If repairs or cleaning are unfinished, early visitors may see a home that does not present as well as it could. We decide what needs attention before setting a launch date.

Pricing on hope

A price above what comparable sales support may limit interest. We review the evidence before listing, and revisit the price if buyer response calls for it.

Marketing that stops at the MLS

Entering a listing into the system is the bare minimum. It is not a marketing plan.

Start to finish

How your sale actually goes

Six stages, grouped into the three phases every sale passes through.

Before market

Stages 01–03

01

The walkthrough — free, no commitment

We walk the house together, room by room. I tell you what buyers in your price range will notice, what is worth fixing, and—just as important—what to leave alone. You get a written list. You are under no obligation to list with me, or at all.

02

A price range based on relevant sales

I review recent sales of comparable homes, widening the search when needed. We look at condition, lot, updates and current competition, and I explain how the evidence supports the range.

03

Prep, stage and shoot

Repairs coordinated with contractors I trust, staging advice using what you already own, then the photography and video agreed in your plan. Everything ready before day one.

On market

Stages 04–05

04

Launch, loudly

Your listing is shared through the MLS and participating listing sites, with additional promotion as agreed in your marketing plan. That can include paid social media ads aimed at buyers searching your area, an email to my agent network, a preview for other agents, and promoted open houses. We agree how showings will work around your schedule, and I pass on what buyers and their agents say, so we can see how the home is being received and adjust if needed.

05

Offers, negotiated properly

Every offer explained beyond the price: how solid the buyer’s financing is, whether the appraisal is likely to be below the offer price, what issues the inspection could raise, and the dates. The highest number is not always the best offer, and you will know why before you sign.

Under contract

Stage 06

06

Contract to keys

Inspection, appraisal, lender, title. If the inspection leads to repair requests, we review them together and decide how to respond before the contract deadline. If an appraisal comes in below the contract price, we look at the report and the options your contract allows. I chase every deadline so you do not have to, and you hear from me before you have to wonder.

Every sale starts with price. I review recent sales and your home’s details myself, then talk you through the range.

Local context

Selling across the Toledo area

Toledo is not one market. The homes yours is measured against depend on where it is and what it is.

Toledo and the surrounding communities

A Toledo city neighborhood, an older suburb and a newer Wood County subdivision can be a short drive apart and still compete with very different homes. The comparable sales and competing listings I review come from the part of the market your home actually sits in, and I will tell you when there are too few to lean on.
