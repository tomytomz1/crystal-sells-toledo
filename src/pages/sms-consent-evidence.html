Actual SMS opt-in flow

The consumer form at crystalsellstoledo.com/home-value is a two-step form. The SMS consent checkbox is on step 2.

  1. The consumer visits https://crystalsellstoledo.com/home-value.
  2. Step 1 asks for the property address the consumer wants valued.
  3. Step 2 asks for contact information — name, email, phone, selling timeline and property condition — and then displays a section headed “How may Crystal follow up? Optional.”
  4. SMS consent is represented by its own separate checkbox inside that section.
  5. That SMS checkbox starts unchecked, is optional, is not required to submit the real estate inquiry, is separate from the automated / AI voice permission, and must be affirmatively selected by the consumer before any text message is permitted.
  6. The consumer can submit the underlying real estate inquiry with both communication-permission checkboxes left unchecked, and Crystal still replies by email or phone about what was asked for.

Leaving these unticked will not affect your request — Crystal will still reply by email or phone about what you asked for.

Replied STOP to Crystal’s texts before? Ticking the text-message box again does not restart them by itself. Reply START from that same phone as well — texts resume only once both are on record.

Both boxes are shown unchecked because both ship unchecked. Neither is pre-selected, neither is required, and the two are separate permissions: agreeing to text messages does not grant permission for automated or AI voice calls, and agreeing to calls does not grant permission for text messages. Each is recorded and revoked on its own.

Where the SMS policies are

The messaging program has its own dedicated policies, scoped to SMS only:

  • SMS Privacy Policy — https://crystalsellstoledo.com/sms-privacy
  • SMS Terms & Conditions — https://crystalsellstoledo.com/sms-terms

The SMS Privacy Policy states that mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and that text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

The separate Website Privacy Policy covers the rest of the website and is not the policy for this messaging campaign.
