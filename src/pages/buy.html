How it works

From first look to front-door key

Get your financing straight first

Not because I want to rush you, but because in this market an offer without a strong pre-approval is not competitive. I can introduce you to local lenders who actually answer on weekends — and you are free to use your own.

Get honest about the neighborhoods

Every part of 43551 trades something for something. Walkability for lot size. New finishes for mature trees. A shorter commute for a busier road. I will tell you the downsides too — that is the part you cannot get from a listing site.

See the good ones early

Real-time alerts the moment something matching hits the MLS, plus a heads-up on what I hear from other agents before it lists. Same-day showings whenever I can arrange them.

Write an offer that wins without overpaying

Price is one lever out of many. Inspection scope, appraisal gap language, closing date, possession, escalation — used well, these win homes against higher bids. Used carelessly, they cost you dearly.

Inspect, negotiate, close

I am there for the inspection, I help you separate the deal-breakers from the punch list, and I stay on the lender and title company until the keys are in your hand.

First time buying?

You are allowed to ask the basic questions.

Escrow, earnest money, PMI, contingencies, closing costs — nobody is born knowing any of it.

I will explain every document before you sign it, tell you what is normal and what is not, and never make you feel silly for asking. Ohio has first-time buyer programs and down payment assistance that a lot of people never hear about; we will check whether you qualify.

And if the honest answer is that you should wait six months and save a little more — I will tell you that too.
