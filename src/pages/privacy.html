Privacy & terms

The short version: what you send through a form is used to answer you and nothing else, and it is never sold. This page also names every third-party service this site loads, because some of them see something about your visit whether or not you ever fill in a form.

Written in plain language, and not legal advice. Effective and last updated September 2, 2026.

What is collected when you submit a form

The valuation and contact forms collect what you type: your first and last name, your email address, your phone number if you give one, the property address you asked about, when you might sell, the condition of the property, and any message or notes you write.

Submitted alongside it, automatically, is the context of the enquiry: the page you landed on, the page you submitted from, the site that referred you, any campaign tags in the link you arrived through (UTM parameters and Google, Meta or Microsoft click identifiers), when you first arrived, when you submitted, and an internal submission reference. This is how Crystal can tell which of her efforts actually reach people; none of it identifies you beyond what you typed.

Your IP address is briefly held in the server’s memory to limit how many submissions can come from one address in a short window. It is not written to the contact record and is not stored beyond that window.

Why it is processed

To prepare and send what you asked for, and to follow up about that request. If you have asked for a valuation and told me you are simply curious, you will receive the valuation and then be left alone.

The lawful basis, in plain terms, is that you asked for something and this is what is needed to give it to you — plus a legitimate interest in understanding, at an aggregate level, which pages and campaigns are working.

What runs on this site

Your information is never sold or traded, and it is not shared for anyone else’s marketing. It is handled by the service providers listed below, each of which processes it on Crystal’s behalf and under its own privacy terms.

Vercel — hosting and Web Analytics

The site is hosted on Vercel, which necessarily sees the requests your browser makes to it. Vercel Web Analytics counts visits: it is cookieless and records the page visited, a referrer, and a coarse country, device and browser type. It does not build a profile of you and cannot follow you to other sites.

Google Analytics 4 — on the live site

The live site loads Google Analytics 4. Preview and local builds do not. It records the pages you view, a coarse location derived from your IP address, your device and browser, and a small set of events this site defines itself — clicking a call or email link, starting or completing a step of a form, and whether a submission succeeded or failed. The events record that the action happened, not what you typed.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) so repeat visits can be told apart. There is no cookie banner on this site today. If you would rather not be counted, you can block cookies for this site in your browser, use private browsing, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. Nothing on the site is withheld if you do.

Google Fonts

Typefaces are served from Google Fonts. Loading them means Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. No cookie is set by the font files.

Google Places — address suggestions

Where address autocomplete is switched on, the characters you type into a property-address field are sent to Google’s Places service to return matching addresses. Nothing else on the form is sent, and nothing is sent until you start typing in that field. If the feature is not configured, the field is an ordinary text box and Google sees nothing.

HubSpot — contact records

When you submit a form, the details above are sent to HubSpot, the customer relationship system where Crystal keeps her contact records and follow-ups. A contact record is created or, if you have written before, updated — matched on your email address so you do not end up as two people — and the enquiry is recorded against it as a dated submission so an earlier message is never overwritten by a later one.

Anyone else

Beyond the providers above, your information is shared only where it is necessary to complete a transaction you have asked for — a title company, lender or inspector you have chosen to work with — or where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Contact records are kept while you are working with Crystal or might reasonably want to, and for as long afterwards as the record-keeping obligations that apply to a licensed real estate brokerage require. They are deleted on request, subject to those obligations.

Analytics data is kept according to the retention period configured in each provider’s own console; it is not tied to your name or email in either one. The IP address used for submission rate limiting is discarded within minutes.

Asking what is held, or asking for deletion

Email crystal@crystalsellstoledo.com from — or naming — the address you used, and say whether you want a copy of what is held, a correction, or deletion. Crystal will confirm and act on it as promptly as she reasonably can. If a request cannot be met in full, you will be told which part and why.

Analytics records are not linked to your name or email, so a deletion request covers the contact record and the enquiry detail held with it.
