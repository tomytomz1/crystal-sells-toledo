What is collected

Only what you type into a form on this site — typically your name, email address, phone number, the property address you asked about, and any message you write. Nothing is collected about you unless you choose to submit it.

How it is used

To prepare and send what you requested, and to follow up about that request. If you have asked for a valuation and told me you are simply curious, you will receive the valuation and then be left alone.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, except where it is necessary to complete a transaction you have asked for — for example a title company, lender or inspector you have chosen to work with — or where the law requires it. Your information is never sold or traded to third parties.
