Privacy & terms

The short version: we use your form details to respond to your request and manage related follow-up. We also record how you found the site, so Crystal can understand which pages and campaigns lead to inquiries. Your details are never sold. This page also names every third-party service this site loads, because some of them see something about your visit whether or not you ever fill in a form.

Effective and last updated September 4, 2026.

What is collected when you submit a form

The home valuation, Seller Strategy Review and contact forms collect the details you enter. Depending on the form, these include your first and last name, your email address, your phone number if you give one, the property address you asked about, when you might sell, the condition of the property, and any message or notes you write.

Submitted alongside it, automatically, is the context of the inquiry: the page you landed on, the page you submitted from, the site that referred you, campaign information in the link you followed (including Google, Meta or Microsoft click identifiers), when you first arrived, when you submitted, and a reference number for your request. This is how Crystal can tell which of her efforts reach people. This information is stored with your inquiry.

The submission service temporarily holds IP addresses in server memory to limit repeated requests. Expired entries are cleared during later requests or when the server instance ends. This service does not add them to your contact record. Hosting providers may separately process request information under their own terms.

Why it is processed

To prepare and send what you asked for, and to follow up about that request. If you asked for a valuation, the form records how far along you are, including a “Just curious about the value” option, and that is stored with your inquiry.

We also record how you found the site so Crystal can understand which pages and campaigns lead to inquiries.

What runs on this site

Your information is never sold or traded, and it is not shared for anyone else’s marketing. It is handled by the service providers listed below, each of which processes it on Crystal’s behalf and under its own privacy terms.

Vercel — hosting and Web Analytics

The site is hosted on Vercel, which necessarily sees the requests your browser makes to it. Vercel Web Analytics counts visits: it is cookieless and records the page visited, a referrer, and a coarse country, device and browser type. It does not build a profile of you and cannot follow you to other sites.

Google Analytics 4 — on the live site

The live site loads Google Analytics 4. Preview and local builds do not. It records the pages you view, a coarse location derived from your IP address, your device and browser, and a small set of events this site defines itself — clicking a call or email link, starting or completing a step of a form, and whether a submission succeeded or failed. Form-event tracking records the action, not the contents of your form. Where an event records a link destination, a mailto address is recorded without its subject or message.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) so repeat visits can be told apart. There is no cookie banner on this site today. Private browsing does not necessarily prevent analytics collection. Google offers an Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Browser or content-blocking settings may also limit tracking, depending on how they are configured. Nothing on the site is withheld if you use them.

Google Fonts

Typefaces are served from Google Fonts. Loading them means Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. No cookie is set by the font files.

Google Places — address suggestions

When this feature is enabled, Google’s Maps script loads with the page and receives normal connection information, such as your IP address. As you type in the address field, the entered address text is sent to Google to return suggestions. Nothing else on the form is sent. If the feature is disabled, the field is an ordinary text box and the site does not make those Maps or Places requests.

HubSpot — contact records

When you submit a form, the details above are sent to HubSpot, the customer relationship system where Crystal keeps her contact records and follow-ups. HubSpot stores your contact details and a dated record of your inquiry. If you contact Crystal again using the same email address, the new inquiry is added to your contact history rather than replacing what came before.

Anyone else

Beyond the providers above, your information is shared only where it is necessary to complete a transaction you have asked for — a title company, lender or inspector you have chosen to work with — or where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Contact records are kept while Crystal is working with you or following up on your request, and afterward for as long as the record-keeping obligations that apply to a licensed real estate brokerage require. They are deleted on request, subject to those obligations.

Analytics data is kept according to the retention period configured in each provider’s own console. The IP address used for submission rate limiting is held only in temporary server memory, and expired entries are cleared during later requests or when the server instance ends.

This site also uses your browser’s local storage to remember your first visit and the campaign information in the link you followed, even if you never submit a form. That record stays on your device until it is cleared from the browser; this site does not currently set an automatic expiration for it. Clearing site data for this site removes it.

Asking what is held, or asking for deletion

Email crystal@crystalsellstoledo.com from — or naming — the address you used, and say whether you want a copy of what is held, a correction, or deletion. Crystal may need to confirm your identity before acting on the request. She will explain what can be provided or removed, and what must be retained under applicable requirements. If a request cannot be met in full, you will be told which part and why.

A request may cover information held in contact records and by service providers. Crystal will review what can be located and removed, and explain any limits or required retention. Analytics events do not record your name or email, but some carry the same reference number as your inquiry.
