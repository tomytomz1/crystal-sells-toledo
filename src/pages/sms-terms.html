SMS consent is collected through a separate, optional checkbox on Crystal Sells Toledo web forms. The checkbox starts unchecked and is not required to submit the real estate inquiry or receive service. Consent applies only to this Crystal Sells Toledo SMS program and is not transferable to another business, affiliate, campaign or marketing list.

Submitting a later web form does not silently override a prior STOP or other do-not-text request. A stopped number remains blocked until a deliberate re-opt-in is completed.
