Cloudflare Turnstile — checking that a person filled the form

The forms on this site use Cloudflare Turnstile, which helps tell a person filling in a form apart from an automated script. When you open a page containing one of these forms, Cloudflare’s script loads and receives normal connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, together with signals about how the browser behaves. It does not receive anything you type into the form.

When you submit, the site sends the resulting verification token to Cloudflare to confirm it is genuine. That check sends the token only — not your name, email address, phone number, property address, message or IP address — and the token itself is never stored, never added to Crystal’s contact records and never included in any email. Most visitors are verified without doing anything; occasionally Cloudflare asks you to confirm with a single click.{{turnstileEnforcementNote}}
