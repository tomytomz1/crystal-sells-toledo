Text messages and automated calls

The forms on this site have two optional tick boxes: one to allow text messages about your inquiry, and a separate one to allow calls that may use automated technology and an artificial, prerecorded, or AI-generated voice. Both start unticked. Neither is required, and leaving both unticked has no effect on your request or on the help you receive.

If you tick a box, what is recorded is the choice itself, the exact wording you were shown, a version identifier for that wording, the phone number you provided, the page you were on, the time, and a reference number for the submission. That record is stored with your inquiry in HubSpot. Your IP address is not stored as part of it — addresses are held briefly in server memory to limit repeated submissions, as described above, and are not added to your contact record.

Permission is tied to the number you gave at the time. If your number changes, the earlier permission does not follow you to the new one.

Twilio — text message delivery

When text messages are sent, Twilio is the provider that delivers them. Twilio receives the recipient phone number, the message content, and delivery metadata such as timestamps and delivery status, in order to send the message and report what happened to it. If you reply, Twilio receives that reply and passes it on.

Retell AI — automated voice calls

When automated calls are made, Retell AI is the provider that places and conducts them. Retell receives the phone number being called and the content of the conversation in order to run the call. An automated call identifies itself as an AI assistant at the start.

Calls are not recorded or transcribed. If that ever changes, this page will say so before it starts, not after.

What is never done with your mobile information

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. The providers named above receive what they need to deliver a message or place a call you asked for, and nothing is sold or handed to anyone for their own marketing.

These permissions cover your inquiry — replies, valuations you asked for, appointments and reminders, information you requested, and closely related updates. They are not a marketing list. Anything broader would be asked for separately.

Stopping messages and calls

Reply STOP to any text to end the texts, or HELP for contact details. Plain English works too. On a call, saying you do not want to be called again ends the automated calls. You can also email crystal@crystalsellstoledo.com.

Texts and calls are tracked separately, so stopping one does not automatically stop the other — say you want to stop everything and both stop. Once you have opted out, submitting another form does not quietly restart them. The record of your opt-out is kept so it can be honoured, and turning a channel back on takes a deliberate re-opt-in.

Full detail is in the Communications Terms.
